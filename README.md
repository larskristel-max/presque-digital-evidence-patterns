# Evidence patterns / Repères pour examiner les preuves

Practical, bilingual reference notes by [Presque.digital](https://presque.digital/en/), a digital studio exploring how operational questions connect to evidence and human review.

Two worked examples show how to keep missing information visible before making a decision:

| Reference | English | Français | Fictional data |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Device comparison: unknown is not zero | [Compare an offer before ranking its price](docs/comparison.en.md) | [Comparer une offre avant de classer son prix](docs/comparison.fr.md) | [Offers](examples/offers.json) |
| Brewery: production, HACCP and excise | [One brewery question, three separate reviews](docs/brewery.en.md) | [Une question de brasserie, trois examens distincts](docs/brewery.fr.md) | [Batch](examples/batch.json) |

The examples are invented. They contain no live prices, brewery records or private application code. These are evidence-review patterns, not a released application, a purchase recommendation or a complete regulatory checklist.

## Comment lire ces exemples

Ces notes bilingues de Presque.digital montrent comment conserver une information manquante avant de prendre une décision. Les prix, offres et documents de brasserie sont fictifs. Chaque note distingue les faits consignés, les lacunes et l'examen humain encore nécessaire.

## Project context

[OBIA — Operon Brewery Intelligent Assistant](https://presque.digital/en/work/obia/), by Presque.digital, is in a private pilot. Its intended direction is to connect brewery questions to records and prepare a compliance-dossier candidate for review. The [product finder](https://presque.digital/en/journal/unknown-is-not-zero/) is in development. These examples do not demonstrate automatic certification, excise declaration submission or a finished product interface.

## Brewery toolkit / Outils de brasserie

The [complete brewery reference](https://presque.digital/en/resources/brewery-records/#toolkit) includes blank worksheets and worked fictional examples for batch review, quantity reconciliation and recall tracing. Production, HACCP/traceability and excise decisions remain separate. The files here are the same reviewed public templates published on the site; they contain no real brewery records or personal details.
