The three-review arrangement is our illustrative method, not a prescribed official procedure. The fictional cleaning check comes from an invented brewery's own plan; it is not asserted to be a universal critical control point. No rates, deadlines, exemptions, exhaustive obligations or certification claims are provided. Actual applicability depends on the operator and its activity.
