Recorded bottle count: 340 + 120 + 20 + 10 + 10 = 500. The two kegs are outside this bottle-only exercise and require a separate scope assessment. Quantity reconciliation succeeds for the recorded figures; reachability is incomplete because EVENT-01 lacks a usable contact route. An exercise result does not certify recall readiness.
